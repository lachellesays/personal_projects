What's different from today

  • Nothing to save. Scratches and typo fixes save the moment you make them. No "unsaved changes" warnings, and searching never loses an edit.
  • Separate logins for you and your co-admin. Every scratch and fix records who did it and when.
  • "3 new entries since your last download" tells you when the workbook is out of date, and one click shows which ones.
  • Balances on the entries table, so you can spot who owes money while you work.
  • Warnings you can act on: pick the right address variant, jump to the entry, or dismiss it. Today they're just a list.
  • Download history per show, with who downloaded and how many runs it had.
  • Fast to open. Entries are kept in the database and refreshed from JotForm on demand, instead of waiting on JotForm every page load.
  • Works on a phone for quick checks (left), though it's designed for a laptop.
