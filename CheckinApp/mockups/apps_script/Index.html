New trial check-in app: preview

A preview of a new trial check-in app for UKI trials: what exhibitors see on their phones, the gate steward's screen, and the secretary's tools. Everything is clickable, nothing is saved, and the names and dogs are made up. I'd love your feedback on what's confusing, missing, or unnecessary.

Things you can try: switch days and classes, tap Checked in / Scratch, tap START RUN / FINISH on the gate, and drag dogs by the handle on the reorder screen.
